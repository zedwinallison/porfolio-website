Blender Day 1 Tutorial

I created this video as an introductory lesson to introduce Blender to someone who has never touched a 3D software before. Many skills were deployed in the creation of this video, including writing, editing, animation, illustration, voice-over, and expertise in structuring an entertaining video.

For an educational video, prioritizing entertainment, comedy, and appealing visuals means that the viewer will retain more information. You will notice the sound design in this video as well. Great care went into using sound to capture attention and seamlessly transition between sections.

How to Make Title Cards

This video was created as a study of title cards in film and TV. It breaks down visual design choices, and film editing concepts/practices. My goal was to capture a viewer’s attention and educate while providing entertainment that is engaging, visually interesting, cinematic, and comedic.

State Parks Promotional Videos

This set of promotional films was created on contract with Wyoming State Parks to provide information about several historic sites. These films were directed, filmed, and edited by me, in accordance with certain information that State Parks wanted to communicate. They are consistently styled, and fit as a set of three, with identical formatting, visuals, and sound design.
